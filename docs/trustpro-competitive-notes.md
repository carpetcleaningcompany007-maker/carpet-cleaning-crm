# TrustPro comparison notes

Reviewed: 10 September 2026

## Observed price structure

- Core solo plan: $29/month for one user.
- AI Receptionist: separate $69/month add-on.
- Core plus AI Receptionist: $98/month before other usage costs.
- Team plan: $149/month for up to five users.
- Team Pro: $299/month for up to fifteen users.
- Marketing communications: prepaid balance, with message segments at $0.04 each.
- The marketing balance also funds TrustPro business-number renewal.
- Payment processing: published as 3.1% standard, or 4.1% for instant payouts.

## What TrustPro does well

- Clearly separates Requests, Estimates, Jobs, Invoices, Customers, Schedule, price items, photos and communications.
- Request record can include source, service details, photos, priced items, instructions and preferred times.
- Estimate builder supports editable line items, quantity, price, discounts, tax, deposits, customer message, private notes and files.
- Schedule supports jobs, tasks, drag-in requests and an unscheduled-work area.
- Includes customer vetting and payment-history checks as its distinctive feature.
- Has branded payment links, deposits, invoices and automatic reminders.

## What to take into Carpet Cleaning Manager

1. Optional deposit on each quote: none, fixed amount or percentage.
2. A clear payment-link state: deposit requested, paid, balance due, paid in full.
3. Simple private job notes and file attachments alongside the customer-visible quote.
4. One clean route from enquiry to quote, booking, job, invoice and repeat follow-up.
5. Separate communications billing from the core CRM subscription in any future commercial plan.
6. Keep a simple, obvious price book while retaining the carpet-specific pricing engine.

## Where Carpet Cleaning Manager can be stronger

- UK carpet-cleaning language, business details and payment expectations.
- Cleaning-method-aware prices: professional deep clean, standard wand clean, low moisture, bonnet, Rotowash, dry compound and upholstery.
- First-room/additional-room rates, three-room specials, stairs and landing rules, and upholstery seat pricing.
- Customer form answers that feed directly into an editable quote.
- A phone-first home screen which tells the cleaner exactly what to do next.
- Clear delivery status and human approval before important messages go out.

## Product gaps to finish before selling widely

- Reliable inbound text and email capture.
- Card payment and deposit integration.
- Multi-business accounts, user roles and data separation.
- Reliable, tested scheduled follow-ups that never duplicate messages.
- Communication credit/balance handling if a dedicated number, SMS campaigns or auto-replies are offered.
