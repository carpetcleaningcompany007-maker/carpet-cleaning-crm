Your team's quick guide

From first enquiry to a happy customer

A clear next step, with you in control of the price and the message.

Open enquiries
  1. 01

    Notice the enquiry

    Check when it arrived, the customer's details and the page they came from, where recorded. Read the message status before making contact.

    On Today: stop a queued acknowledgement, send it now during contact hours, or take over personally.
  2. 02

    Get the cleaning list

    Ask for each room, hall, stairs and landing separately, plus sofa sizes, upholstery type, the property address and useful photos.

    Keep the customer's exact requirements together in their record.
  3. 03

    Choose the right package Optional

    Ask whether their priority is the lowest cost, the most thorough clean, or a balance. Explain suitable methods and what each package includes.

    Already said “the best possible job”? Skip the question and prepare the appropriate quote.
  4. 04

    Prepare and check the quote

    List the work item by item. Check the quantities, prices, total and customer address. Resolve missing information before sending.

    Use the quote calculator today. Automatic drafts from customer replies are planned, not active yet.
  5. 05

    Agree, then book

    Send the checked quote, record the customer's agreement and arrange the job. If there is no reply, log your contact attempt and set a follow-up date.

    “Called — no answer”, “Left voicemail” and “Message sent — waiting” keep the history clear.
  6. 06

    Invoice and follow through

    Prepare the invoice from the agreed work. Check customer details, the amount and payment instructions before sending. Keep payment and follow-up records up to date.

    A quotation proposes the work and price. An invoice requests payment.

Make it your business

Save your own company name, contact details, logo and email signature in Settings. Review your prices in the quote calculator.

Planned: a reusable quote footer with your payment instructions and bank details, editable for each quote.

Know what has actually happened

A queued message has not been sent. “Accepted” means the provider accepted it; it does not confirm delivery. Check failures and the contact history before trying again.

The planned reply-to-quote workflow will prepare a draft for your review. It will not approve prices or send that quote for you.

Suggested wording: ask about their priorities

“Thanks for sending those details over. To help me put together the right cleaning package, what matters most to you: keeping the cost as low as possible, getting the most thorough deep clean, or finding a good balance between the two? If you have a budget in mind, please let me know.”

Use this only when their preference is unclear. Adapt it to your own business and the conversation.
